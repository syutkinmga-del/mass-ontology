- Independent ROBOT/HermiT reasoning and negative schema/data mutations.

- No operational ROC/VTS authority model, COLREG compliance inference, navigation safety certification or production ship control.

- No full BFO/CCO imported alignment; external standards are pinned/referenced under the T-04 policy.
