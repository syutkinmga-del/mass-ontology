## 3. Structural Invariants Without Standalone ConstraintDefinition

The baseline validation includes SK1 and SK2 even though they are not represented as standalone 
`ConstraintDefinition` elements.

### SK1

`MASSOperationalSystem` contains exactly one subject MASS.

This property is enforced and validated structurally.

### SK2

`RemoteOperationsCentre` is referenced / associated with the operational system and is not modeled as a 
composite part of `MaritimeAutonomousSurfaceShip`.

This property is enforced and validated structurally.

Therefore:

**SK1–SK16 Regression: PASS**

remains the correct validation statement for the released baseline.
